The Chilean coffee market is unique in Latin America. Unlike neighboring producers, Chile relies entirely on imports[2]. This creates a dynamic market with:

  • Strong instant coffee preference due to urban lifestyles
  • Rapid third-wave specialty coffee emergence in Santiago
  • Increasing demand for premium, ethically sourced beans
  • Balance of international chains and local roasters

Specialty Growth: Chile specialty coffee market growing at 10.07% CAGR (2025-2033)[7]—nearly double overall market rate.

Third-Wave Movement

Santiago and Valparaíso lead third-wave transformation[8]:

  • Artisanal Focus: High-quality beans, direct trade, meticulous brewing
  • Consumer Education: Growing awareness of varieties and methods
